A Closer Look at the DQ Food Menu

Dairy Queen is not just about ice cream. The chain started as a treat shop but has grown into a full-service fast-food restaurant. Many locations are called "Grill & Chill" because they serve both hot food and cold treats. The food menu has changed over the years to include more items and flavors. Today, you can find a solid mix of classic American fast food.

The menu is simple and easy to read. You have burgers, chicken, hot dogs, and sides. Then, you have the drinks and desserts. Most meals come in combos. A combo usually includes the main item, fries, and a drink. This is often a better value than buying items alone. The Dairy Queen menu with prices shows these combos clearly, making it simple to compare costs.

The quality of the food is good for fast food. DQ uses 100% seasoned real beef for their burgers. Their chicken strips are made with all-white meat. These details show that DQ cares about the taste and quality of their food.

Burgers: Stackburgers and Classic Choices

Burgers are a big part of the Dairy Queen food menu. They call their signature burgers "Stackburgers." You can usually choose a single, double, or triple patty. This lets you pick the size that fits your hunger and your budget. The Dairy Queen menu with prices varies based on the number of patties you choose.

The Original Cheeseburger

This is the classic you know and love. It comes with a beef patty, melted cheese, pickles, ketchup, and mustard on a toasted bun. It's simple, tasty, and very affordable. A single Original Cheeseburger starts around $6.23 for the burger alone. If you want a combo with fries and a drink, the price goes up to about $10.79. For a double, the price increases, and a triple will cost more.

The Bacon Cheese Deluxe Stackburger

This burger is for people who want it all. It has one or more beef patties, two types of melted cheese (Sharp American and White Cheddar), crispy bacon, tomato, onion, lettuce, and pickles. It also has ketchup and mayo. This burger is packed with flavor and is quite filling. A double combo costs around $14.39. This price includes the drink and fries.

The Flamethrower Stackburger

If you like spicy food, this is the burger for you. The Flamethrower has the DQ FlameThrower sauce, which gives it a nice kick. It also has Pepper Jack cheese, jalapeno bacon, tomato, and lettuce. This burger is full of heat and flavor. A single Flamethrower combo starts at about $14.99. It's a popular choice for those who want something different from a regular burger.

The Backyard Bacon Ranch Stackburger

This burger is all about smoky and creamy flavors. It comes with House-Made Hidden Valley Ranch dressing, Honey BBQ sauce, crispy onion rings, and applewood smoked bacon. The combination of ranch and BBQ is very popular. A combo meal for this burger costs around $14.15.

BeltBuster and Hungr-Buster

At some DQ locations, you might see these names on the menu. The Hungr-Buster is a 1/4 pound beef patty with classic toppings like lettuce, tomatoes, onions, pickles, and mustard. The BeltBuster has two 1/4 pound patties. These are simple, no-fuss burgers that are easy on the wallet. A Hungr Buster combo is around $10.69. The BeltBuster combo is a bit more, at about $13.79.

Chicken Baskets and Sandwiches

If you prefer chicken over beef, Dairy Queen has many great options. Their chicken strips are very popular. They are made from 100% all-tenderloin white meat. This means they are juicy and tender. You can get them in a basket or on a sandwich.

Chicken Strip Baskets

This is one of the most ordered items at Dairy Queen. The basket comes with a choice of 4 or 6 chicken strips. They are served with crispy fries, Texas toast, and your choice of dipping sauce. Many people love the brown gravy. You can also get them "Sauced & Tossed" with flavors like Honey BBQ.

Prices for the chicken strip basket start around $11.39 for a 4-piece basket and go up for the 6-piece. It is a hearty meal that is very satisfying. It is easy to see why this is a customer favorite.

Chicken Strip Sandwiches

You can also get your chicken strips on a bun. The Original Chicken Strip Sandwich has two seasoned chicken strips on a bun with lettuce, tomato, and mayo. The Spicy Chicken Strip Sandwich is the same, but it has the FlameThrower sauce for some heat. These sandwiches are a lighter option compared to the full basket.

A chicken strip sandwich costs about $9.99 for the sandwich alone. A combo with fries and a drink comes to around $13.55. It is a great choice for a filling lunch that is not too heavy.

Crispy Chicken Sandwich & Grilled Chicken Sandwich

Some locations also offer a breaded chicken breast sandwich. This is a classic fried chicken fillet with lettuce, tomatoes, and dressing. For a healthier option, there is the Grilled Chicken Sandwich. It has a juicy grilled chicken breast with the same fresh toppings. Prices vary, but these are usually in the $6 $9 range.

Hot Dogs, Fish, and More

Dairy Queens menu goes beyond burgers and chicken. They serve other items that add variety to the menu. Hot dogs are a popular choice, especially for a quick and simple meal.

Classic Hot Dogs

DQ offers classic hot dogs, served with your choice of toppings. You can get them plain or with cheese. For something more filling, try the Chili Cheese Dog. It is topped with warm chili and melted cheese. A Chili Cheese Dog combo costs about $10.67. It's a tasty choice for hot dog lovers.

Wild Alaskan Fish Sandwich

For a seafood option, DQ has the Wild Alaskan Fish Sandwich. It features a crispy breaded Pollock filet with lettuce and tartar sauce on a toasted bun. It's a good option if you want something different from meat. The combo meal is priced around $13.19.

Salads

If you are looking for a lighter meal, Dairy Queen has salads. The Crispy Chicken Strips Salad Bowl is a popular choice. It has crispy chicken strips on a bed of lettuce with tomatoes and bacon. It's a great way to enjoy the taste of DQ chicken in a lighter way. The price is competitive with other fast-food salads.

Sides and Snacks

No meal is complete without some good sides. Dairy Queen has several options to choose from.

  • Fries DQ serves hot, crispy, and tasty fries. You can get them as a side or as part of a combo meal.
  • Onion Rings These are another great option. They are crispy and golden on the outside with a sweet onion inside.
  • Pretzel Sticks with Zesty Queso These are soft, hot pretzel sticks served with a warm queso dipping sauce. Perfect for sharing.
  • Cheese Curds At some locations, you can get battered and fried cheese curds. They are warm, melty, and crispy.
  • Poutine In Canada, Dairy Queen offers poutine. This is fries topped with cheese curds and gravy. It is a very popular dish.

Drinks and Blended Treats

Dairy Queen has a full drink menu to go with your meal.

Classic Soft Drinks

You can get all your favorite fountain drinks. Options include Pepsi, Diet Pepsi, Starry, Mountain Dew, and Mug Root Beer. A regular soft drink costs about $2.99. A large drink is around $3.59. These are included in combo meals, which is a good deal.

MISTY Slush and Freeze

For something more refreshing, try a MISTY Slush. It is a sweet and slushy drink available in flavors like Blue Raspberry, Cherry, and Lemon Lime. The MISTY Freeze is similar but blended with DQ soft serve. This makes it creamy and smooth. Prices for these start around $4.19 for a small slush.

Shakes and Malts

DQ is famous for its shakes and malts. They are made with milk and creamy DQ vanilla soft serve. They are blended until thick and delicious. A classic shake costs about $6.23. You can choose from flavors like Chocolate, Strawberry, and Caramel. A malt is similar but has malt powder for a slightly different taste.

MooLatté and Smoothies

If you need a caffeine boost, try a MooLatté. It's coffee blended with DQ soft serve and ice. It comes in flavors like Mocha and Caramel. For a fruitier option, there are Premium Fruit Smoothies. They are made with real fruit and low-fat yogurt. These are a tasty and slightly healthier choice.

Blizzard Treats and Cakes

Of course, we cannot forget the Blizzard. This is Dairy Queen's most famous dessert. It's soft serve blended with candies, cookies, and other sweet mix-ins.

Popular Blizzard Flavors

There are many Blizzard flavors to choose from. The OREO Blizzard is a classic favorite. The Chocolate Chip Cookie Dough Blizzard is very popular too. It has cookie dough pieces blended with fudge.

  • REESES Peanut Butter Cup Blizzard
  • M&MS Milk Chocolate Candies Blizzard
  • Snickers Blizzard
  • Choco Brownie Extreme Blizzard

The menu also has special flavors that change with the seasons. For example, the Pumpkin Pie Blizzard is available in the fall. Blizzard treats cost around $5.53 for a small size. They are a great way to end your meal.

DQ Cakes and Treats

Dairy Queen also makes celebration cakes. They have the classic fudge and crunch center surrounded by vanilla and chocolate soft serve. The Blizzard Cake is another option. It combines Blizzard flavors with the classic cake design.

For smaller treats, try the DQ Sandwich. It is vanilla soft serve between two chocolate wafers. The Dilly Bar is vanilla soft serve dipped in a chocolate or butterscotch coating. These are great for a quick, single-serving treat.
